# Code of conduct

This is a small personal project. A few rules keep it pleasant to work on.

- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person means well and may be writing in a second language.
- Talk about the code, the data and the idea, not about the person.
- Disagree with reasons and examples. Accept a decision once it is made.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks or unwanted personal attention, here or in
  private messages that follow from here.
- Respect privacy. Do not post anyone's locations, tracks, addresses or account details, including
  your own (see `SECURITY.md`).

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and
may block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub profile. Reports
are kept confidential.
